# nomi · brand story

A small one. What nomi is, how it feels, and how it speaks. Check new ideas against it.

## The name

**nomi** (鑿) is Japanese for a chisel: a small hand tool that shapes wood a little at a time, by hand, with patience.

**.earth** is the ground.

Together, **nomi.earth** is a maker's patch of earth: a quiet place where things get shaped, slowly, by hand. It never needs explaining on the site. It just feels right.

## The one line

> i design and build things.

Everything else hangs off this. Product designer by trade; on screens and off: apps, furniture, paper, food, the odd workshop.

## How it feels

A raked sand garden. Warm, light sand, fine lines drawn by hand, one small green bud where the lines turn. Calm, unhurried, handmade. Nothing loud, nothing techy.

- **Ink on sand.** Colour only where something is alive: the bud, and each project's own mark.
- **Things still growing sit faded.** They come up to full when you look at them.
- **Space is part of it.** Few words, lots of sand.

## How it speaks

- lowercase, first person: "i".
- Short, warm, plain. Say what it is, never pitch it.
- Honest about where things are: "building", "i'm no master, so we learn together."
- No long dashes. A full stop, comma or colon instead.

## The marks

- **the wordmark**: just **nomi**, where the first letter is the raked n itself, followed by "omi" in Fraunces. The icon and the name are one thing. The raked lines are a touch heavier than in the icon, to sit with the serif letters. Files in `docs/brand/`: `wordmark.svg`, `wordmark-earth.svg` (with ".earth" in grey, for share images and print), `wordmark-on-ink.svg`. Not on the site yet.
- **the raked n**: the brand icon. Three lines drawn through sand in the shape of an n: the rake, the name and the garden in one mark. Ink lines on a sand square. Files: `favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, `apple-touch-icon.png`.
- **永**: the brush workshop. It means "forever". Every brush student starts with it: it holds all eight basic strokes.
- **the bud**: something just starting to grow. It sits where the sand lines turn.

## The look

Small, calm and warm. Nothing pure black, nothing pure white.

### Colour

| | colour | used for |
|---|---|---|
| sand | `#FAF6F1` | the page, and the contact card |
| deeper sand | `#F6EFE7` | the icon square |
| root sand | `#DCCFC2` | the "beta" and "building" pills (the colour of the bud's root), with line ink text |
| field cream | `#FDFAF6` | form boxes |
| ink | `#141413` | the marks only: the raked n, squared's mark, 永 |
| soft ink | `#3A3631` | the name, titles, the send button, text you type, hovers |
| line ink | `#57524B` | the one line under the name |
| grey | `#6F6A63` | every small line of text (5 : 1 on sand) |
| line | `#E7DDD3` | raked sand lines, tile outlines, dotted empty tiles, rules |
| focus | `#B3AA9F` | the outline of the form box you're typing in |
| bud | `#7FA165` / `#8CAE70` | only the bud and things that are alive |

Three steps of text, dark to light: soft ink, line ink, grey. Never more.

### Type

- **Fraunces** (450) for anything that speaks: the name 35px, the one line 19px, titles 20 to 22px, blurb titles 15px.
- **Inter** for everything small: 14px for text and links, 12px for the footer and labels, 9px (600) for pills.
- Lowercase throughout. No underlines.

### The sand

- **Grain:** a fine, even sandpaper grain over the whole page. Its strength is one number in the code (0.6 now; 0.8 is stronger).
- **Light:** soft sunlight from the top left, a little depth at the bottom right. Stronger and wider on phones.
- **Raked lines:** quarter circles from the top right corner, and an S-shaped sweep along the bottom. Fine, flat, hand-drawn, 12 apart. Never 3d, never shaded. They never cross the text.
- **The bud:** sits in the bowl of the bottom sweep, where the lines turn.

### Pieces

- **Tiles:** rounded squares (13px corners), clear inside, a fine line outline. Live work at full strength; things still being built at half, coming up to full on hover or tap. Empty tiles are dotted.
- **Pills:** root sand, line ink text. "building" has a small slow-pulsing dot.
- **Blurbs:** hover a tile (or tap on a phone) and its name and note fade in, in place of the empty row, so nothing moves.
- **Links:** quiet grey text ("about", "get in touch"), soft ink on hover.
- **Contact card:** sand, with cream boxes, a soft focus outline and a soft ink send pill.
- **About:** a short left-aligned note in a narrow column, "← back" above, signed "nomi" in grey Fraunces.

### Marks

- **The raked n:** three arches 9 apart, round ends, stroke about 4% of the icon. Never filled, never coloured.
- **The wordmark:** the raked n sits on the baseline at the height of the small letters. Ink on sand, or sand on ink. ".earth", when shown, is grey. Never set the n in a font: it is always the raked mark.

## What lives here

**lately** shows what's made and what's growing: squared (beta), sianova (building), the brush workshop (building). Empty tiles are room for what's next.

## One rule

One name only: nomi. No other names or personal details, anywhere.
